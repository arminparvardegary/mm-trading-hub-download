Install

  1. Open the file you downloaded and drag MM Trading Hub onto the Applications shortcut. Don’t run it from inside that window.
  2. Open MM Trading Hub from your Applications folder. The first time, macOS says it can’t verify the app, because it isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Click Done, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to MM Trading Hub.
  3. That’s it: it opens on a simulated account, so nothing trades real money until you connect a broker yourself.

If macOS says the app “can’t be opened”

Make sure you downloaded the version for your Mac and that the app is in your Applications folder. Then open Terminal, paste this line, press Return and open the app again:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/MM Trading Hub.app"

Needs macOS 12 or newer. The AI assistant needs Claude Code with your own Claude subscription; brokers and data providers need their own accounts.
